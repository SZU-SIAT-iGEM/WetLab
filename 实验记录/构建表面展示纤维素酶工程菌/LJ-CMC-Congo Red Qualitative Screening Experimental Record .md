# CMC-刚果红平板定性筛选实验记录
## 中文
### 一、基础实验信息
实验名称：INP表面展示工程菌CMC降解能力定性检测
实验日期：2026-08-06——2026-08-09
实验菌株：INP-Cel5L、INP-Cel48S、INP-Cel9K
实验类型：定性实验
补充说明：LB-链霉素平板仅用于前期菌种纯化，未配制纤维素液体富集培养基；染色、脱色全程避光操作；本次共配制2份100 mL CMC培养基，倒板未统一灌注量，琼脂厚度不均。

### 二、实验简要流程
1. 菌悬液制备：LB纯化单菌落用无菌生理盐水重悬，调节OD₆₀₀≈1，三株菌未统一浊度；
2. 平板接种：7 cm CMC平板每块预留1/4空白对照区，采用点样接种，30 ℃倒置避光培养24 h、48 h；
3. 染色脱色（避光）：染色前统一黑色背景拍照；每板添加2–3 mL刚果红避光染色30 min，1 mol/L NaCl避光脱色10–15 min；脱色后分别用白色、黑色背景拍照留存；
4. 对照设置：仅平板内置空白对照，无空载体阴性对照、商业酶阳性对照。

### 三、实验观察结果
1. 菌落生长情况
  培养24 h：仅INP-Cel5L长出清晰可见菌落，Cel48S、Cel9K几乎无肉眼可见菌体；所有空白区域无菌落污染。
  培养48 h：三株工程菌均正常生长；Cel5L、Cel48S菌落长势旺盛，Cel9K菌落偏小、密度更低；空白区保持洁净。

2. 染色显色结果
  平板整体底色呈暗红色，未出现边界清晰的大面积透明水解圈；所有长菌落位置周边出现黄色/淡红色褪色区域，空白对照区无变色。
  菌株降解活性排序：INP-Cel5L ＞ INP-Cel48S ＞ INP-Cel9K。

3. 实验操作缺陷记录
  ① 倒板未标准化，平板琼脂厚薄不一，刚果红吸附、脱色程度差异大，水解区域辨识度下降；
  ② 染色前后拍摄背景不统一，图像底色存在色差，不利于横向对比判读。

### 四、实验结论
1. 三株INP表面展示工程菌均具备微弱CMC内切纤维素酶分泌能力，其中INP-Cel5L降解活性最优；
2. 30 ℃、48 h培养条件下菌株胞外酶分泌量有限，水解显色信号弱，无典型透明水解圈；
3. 后续优化方案：统一平板灌注体积控制琼脂厚度，培养时长延长至72 h以放大酶活差异；
4. 局限性：CMC平板仅反映可溶性底物内切酶活性，需开展Avicel微晶纤维素降解实验完整验证菌株对结晶纤维素的降解能力。

# CMC-Congo Red Qualitative Screening Experimental Record 
## English
### 1 Basic Experimental Information
Experiment Name: Qualitative Detection of CMC Degradation Ability of INP Surface Display Engineered Strains
Date: 2026-08-09
Test Strains: INP-Cel5L, INP-Cel48S, INP-Cel9K
Experiment Type: Qualitative preliminary test
Notes: LB-Streptomycin plates were only used for strain purification; no liquid cellulose enrichment medium was prepared. All staining and decolorization were conducted under dim light. Two batches of 100 mL CMC medium were prepared, pouring volume was not standardized leading to uneven agar thickness.

### 2 Brief Experimental Procedures
1. Bacterial suspension: Single colonies purified on LB plates were resuspended with sterile normal saline to OD₆₀₀ ≈ 1, turbidity normalization was not carried out for three strains.
2. Plate inoculation: Each 7 cm CMC plate reserved a 1/4 built-in blank zone, spot inoculation was adopted, plates were incubated upside down at 30 °C away from light for 24 h and 48 h.
3. Dim-light staining & decolorization: All plates were photographed against black background before staining; 2–3 mL Congo red solution was added for 30 min dark staining, followed by 10–15 min decolorization with 1 mol/L NaCl. Photos were captured on white and black backgrounds after decolorization for archive.
4. Control groups: Only built-in blank control was set, no empty vector negative control or commercial cellulase positive control.

### 3 Experimental Observations
1. Colony growth
24 h incubation: Only INP-Cel5L formed distinct visible colonies; Cel48S and Cel9K showed barely detectable growth. No contamination was observed in blank zones.
48 h incubation: All three strains grew normally. Cel5L and Cel48S exhibited vigorous colony growth, while Cel9K produced smaller and sparser colonies. Blank areas remained clean without colonies.
2. Chromatic results after staining
All plates showed uniform dark red background without large transparent hydrolysis halos with sharp boundaries. Yellow or pale faded zones appeared around all colony spots, while blank control zones kept unchanged red color.
CMC degradation activity ranking: INP-Cel5L > INP-Cel48S > INP-Cel9K.
3. Records of experimental defects
① Unstandardized plate pouring caused inconsistent agar thickness, resulting in uneven Congo red adsorption and decolorization, reducing distinguishability of hydrolysis zones.
② Shooting backgrounds were inconsistent before and after staining, background color difference of images interfered with horizontal comparison and result interpretation.

### 4 Experimental Conclusions
1. All three INP surface display strains can secrete weak CMC endoglucanase, and INP-Cel5L possesses the highest degradation activity.
2. Limited extracellular enzyme secretion under incubation condition of 30 °C for 48 h leads to faint hydrolysis chromatic signal without typical transparent halos.
3. Follow-up optimization: Fix unified pouring volume to control agar thickness, extend incubation time to 72 h to amplify activity difference among strains.
4. Limitation: CMC plate assay only reflects endoglucanase activity on soluble substrate. Avicel microcrystalline cellulose degradation test is required to fully verify strains’ capacity to decompose crystalline cellulose.
