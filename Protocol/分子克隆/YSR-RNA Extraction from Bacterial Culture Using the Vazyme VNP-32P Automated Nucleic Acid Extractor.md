# 使用诺唯赞 VNP-32P 全自动核酸提取仪提取菌液 RNA

## 耗材

- 预封装 RNA 提取试剂板

- 配套 8 联排无菌磁棒套

- Lysis Buffer

- Proteinase K

- TP Buffer

- RDD Buffer

- DNase

- 菌液样本

- Nuclease-free 的枪头、离心管等耗材

## **仪器**

- 诺唯赞 VNP-32P 全自动核酸提取仪

- Nanodrop

## 实验流程

1. **仪器预处理**
   使用前对 VNP-32P 内部进行 75% 乙醇擦拭 + 紫外消毒 30 min，确保无 RNase 污染。取出预封装试剂板，小心撕去铝箔封口膜。

2. **裂解体系配制与加样**
   
   单孔配方： Lysis Buffer 470 μl + Proteinase K 30 μl + TP Buffer 20 μl = 520 μl。
   
   将上述 520 μl 裂解混合液与 100 μl 菌液样本直接混合（总体积 620 μl），加入试剂板第一列的加样孔。

3. **酶消化体系配制与加样**
   单孔配方： RDD Buffer 47 μl + DNase 3 μl = 50 μl。
   
   轻轻混匀后，加入试剂板第二列的酶消化孔。

4. **上机与程序运行**
   将预封装试剂板放入 VNP-32P，注意缺口向上（朝向操作者左上方）。
   
   将磁棒套插入磁棒套架卡槽内，确保卡扣到位。
   
   选择对应的 RNA 提取程序（请勿使用 DNA 提取程序如 DMA4101），启动运行。
   
   仪器将自动完成核酸吸附、杂质洗涤、DNase 消化及 RNA 洗脱。

5. **产物收集与质检**
   程序结束后，从第六列的产物孔中吸取洗脱得到的 RNA。取 1–2 μl 用 Nanodrop 检测浓度与纯度。产物可立即用于逆转录，若不立即使用，可短期保存于 -20℃，或长期保存于 -80℃。

# RNA Extraction from Bacterial Culture Using the Vazyme VNP-32P Automated Nucleic Acid Extractor

## **Materials**

- Pre-packaged RNA extraction reagent plate

- Compatible 8-strip sterile magnetic rod sleeves

- Lysis Buffer

- Proteinase K

- TP Buffer

- RDD Buffer

- DNase

- Bacterial culture sample

- Nuclease-free pipette tips, microcentrifuge tubes, and other consumables

## **Equipment**

- Vazyme VNP-32P Automated Nucleic Acid Extractor

- Nanodrop

## **Procedure**

1. **Instrument Pretreatment**  
   Before use, wipe the interior of the VNP-32P with 75% ethanol and UV sterilize for 30 min to ensure an RNase-free environment. Take out the pre-packaged reagent plate and carefully peel off the aluminum foil sealing film.

2. **Preparation and Loading of Lysis System**
   
   Single-well: Lysis Buffer 470 μl + Proteinase K 30 μl + TP Buffer 20 μl = 520 μl.
   
   Mix the above 520 μl lysis mixture directly with 100 μl of the bacterial culture sample (total volume 620 μl), and load it into the sample loading well in the first column of the reagent plate.

3. **Preparation and Loading of Enzyme Digestion System**  
   Single-well: RDD Buffer 47 μl + DNase 3 μl = 50 μl.
   
   Mix gently and load into the enzyme digestion well in the second column of the reagent plate.

4. **Loading onto Instrument and Program Execution**  
   Place the pre-packaged reagent plate into the VNP-32P, ensuring the notch is facing up (towards the operator's top left).
   
   Insert the magnetic rod sleeves into the slots of the magnetic rod rack and ensure the buckles are securely engaged.
   
   Select the corresponding RNA extraction program (do not use DNA extraction programs such as DMA4101) and start the run.
   
   The instrument will automatically complete nucleic acid adsorption, impurity washing, DNase digestion, and RNA elution.

5. **Product Collection and Quality Inspection**  
   After the program finishes, aspirate the eluted RNA from the product well in the sixth column. Take 1–2 μl to measure concentration and purity using a Nanodrop. The product can be used immediately for reverse transcription. If not used immediately, it can be stored at -20°C for the short term or at -80°C for the long term.
