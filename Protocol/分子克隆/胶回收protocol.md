# HiPure 琼脂糖凝胶DNA回收与反应液纯化

## **耗材**

- HiPure Gel Pure DNA Mini Kit 试剂盒
- 无水乙醇
- 1.5 mL 与 2 mL 离心管
- 移液器及枪头

## **仪器**

- 小型高速离心机
- 恒温水浴锅或金属浴
- NanoDrop 分光光度计

## **实验流程**

**准备工作**
1. 在 Buffer DW2 中加入 4 倍体积的无水乙醇，并室温保存。
2. 将水浴锅温度设至 50~55℃。
3. 低温下 Buffer GDP 可能会有沉淀，使用前须加热至 55℃ 使沉淀溶解。

**实验步骤 1：琼脂糖凝胶 DNA 回收**
1. 配制合适浓度的琼脂糖凝胶，电泳分离 DNA 片段。在紫外灯下快速切下含目的 DNA 片段的凝胶，尽量去除多余的凝胶。
2. 称取凝胶块重量并转移至 1.5 或 2.0 mL 离心管中。按 100 mg 凝胶块相当 100 μL 体积计算，加入 1~3 倍体积的 Buffer GDP。
   - 若凝胶块重量为 200 mg，则加入 200~600 μL Buffer GDP。
   - 若凝胶浓度超过 2.0%，加入 2~3 倍体积的 Buffer GDP。
   - 若处理超过 5 kb 的片段，加 3 倍体积 Buffer GDP 溶胶后，再加入 1 倍体积异丙醇混匀。
3. 50~55℃ 水浴 10~15 分钟，水浴期间颠倒混匀 3 次加速溶胶。短暂离心收集管壁上的液滴。
4. 将 HiPure DNA Mini Column 套在 2 mL 离心管中。把 ≤700 μL 溶胶液转移至柱子中，12,000 × g 离心 30~60 秒。
5. 若溶胶液超过 700 μL，倒弃滤液，把柱子套回 2 mL 离心管中，把剩余的溶胶液转移至柱子中，12,000 × g 离心 30~60 秒。
6. 倒弃滤液，把柱子套回 2 mL 离心管中。加入 300 μL Buffer GDP 至柱子中，静置 1 分钟，12,000 × g 离心 30~60 秒。
7. 倒弃滤液，把柱子套回 2 mL 离心管中。加入 600 μL Buffer DW2（已用无水乙醇稀释）至柱子中，12,000 × g 离心 30~60 秒。
8. 倒弃滤液，把柱子套回 2 mL 离心管中。加入 300 μL Buffer DW2（已用无水乙醇稀释）至柱子中，12,000 × g 离心 2 分钟。
9. 取出柱子时，不要让柱子底部接触滤液。若碰到了液体，倒弃滤液后再离心 1 分钟。对某些敏感应用，需将大部分洗脱液加入连接反应液前，打开柱子的盖子空气干燥 5 分钟以彻底去除乙醇。
10. 把柱子套在 1.5 mL 离心管中，加入 15~30 μL Elution Buffer 至柱子膜中央，放置 2 分钟，12,000 × g 离心 1 分钟。
11. 丢弃柱子，把 DNA 保存于 -20℃。回收大于 3 kb 以上的片段时，最好把 Elution Buffer 预热至 55℃。

**实验步骤 2：从反应液中纯化 DNA**
1. 短暂离心 PCR 产物、酶促反应液或粗制 DNA 产物，用移液枪测量其体积，并转移至灭菌的 1.5 或 2 mL 离心管中。
2. 若样品体积小于 100 μL，用灭菌水调整至 100 μL。高浓度的基因组 DNA 最好用灭菌水稀释至 300 μL，以提高回收效率。
3. 加入等倍体积的 Buffer GDP，颠倒或涡旋混匀。若需回收小于 100 bp 的 DNA 片段，再加入 1.5 倍体积无水乙醇。
4. 将 HiPure DNA 柱子套在收集管中。把混合液转移至 DNA 柱子中，12,000 × g 离心 30~60 秒。
5. 按实验步骤 1 的第 6~8 步进行操作。
6. 使用 NanoDrop 测定纯化后的 DNA 浓度。

# HiPure Gel Pure DNA Mini Kit Protocol

## **Materials and Consumables**

- HiPure Gel Pure DNA Mini Kit
- Anhydrous ethanol
- 1.5 mL and 2 mL centrifuge tubes
- Pipettes and tips

## **Instruments**

- Benchtop microcentrifuge
- Constant-temperature water bath or metal bath
- NanoDrop spectrophotometer

## **Experimental Procedure**

**Preparation**
1. Add 4 volumes of anhydrous ethanol to Buffer DW2 and store at room temperature.
2. Set the water bath temperature to 50–55°C.
3. Buffer GDP may form precipitates at low temperatures; heat to 55°C to dissolve precipitates before use.

**Step 1: Agarose Gel DNA Recovery**
1. Prepare an agarose gel of appropriate concentration and separate DNA fragments by electrophoresis. Excise the gel slice containing the target DNA fragment quickly under UV light, removing as much excess gel as possible.
2. Weigh the gel slice and transfer it to a 1.5 or 2.0 mL centrifuge tube. Calculate 100 mg of gel slice as equivalent to 100 μL volume, and add 1–3 volumes of Buffer GDP.
   - If the gel slice weighs 200 mg, add 200–600 μL of Buffer GDP.
   - If the gel concentration exceeds 2.0%, add 2–3 volumes of Buffer GDP.
   - For fragments larger than 5 kb, add 3 volumes of Buffer GDP to dissolve the gel, then add 1 volume of isopropanol and mix.
3. Incubate in a 50–55°C water bath for 10–15 minutes, inverting to mix 3 times during incubation to accelerate gel dissolution. Briefly centrifuge to collect droplets on the tube wall.
4. Place the HiPure DNA Mini Column into a 2 mL collection tube. Transfer ≤700 μL of the dissolved gel solution to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 30–60 seconds.
5. If the dissolved gel solution exceeds 700 μL, discard the flow-through, place the column back into the 2 mL collection tube, transfer the remaining solution to the column, and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 30–60 seconds.
6. Discard the flow-through and place the column back into the 2 mL collection tube. Add 300 μL of Buffer GDP to the column, let stand for 1 minute, and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 30–60 seconds.
7. Discard the flow-through and place the column back into the 2 mL collection tube. Add 600 μL of Buffer DW2 (diluted with anhydrous ethanol) to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 30–60 seconds.
8. Discard the flow-through and place the column back into the 2 mL collection tube. Add 300 μL of Buffer DW2 (diluted with anhydrous ethanol) to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 2 minutes.
9. Do not let the bottom of the column come into contact with the filtrate when removing it. If contact occurs, discard the filtrate and centrifuge for another 1 minute. For sensitive applications, open the column cap and air-dry for 5 minutes to completely remove ethanol before adding the eluate to the ligation reaction.
10. Place the column into a 1.5 mL centrifuge tube, add 15–30 μL of Elution Buffer to the center of the membrane, let stand for 2 minutes, and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 1 minute.
11. Discard the column and store the DNA at -20°C. For fragments larger than 3 kb, preheating the Elution Buffer to 55°C is recommended.

**Step 2: DNA Purification from Reaction Mixtures**
1. Briefly centrifuge the PCR product, enzymatic reaction mixture, or crude DNA product. Measure the volume with a pipette and transfer to a sterile 1.5 or 2 mL centrifuge tube.
2. If the sample volume is less than 100 μL, adjust to 100 μL with sterile water. High-concentration genomic DNA is best diluted to 300 μL with sterile water to improve recovery efficiency.
3. Add an equal volume of Buffer GDP and mix by inversion or vortexing. If recovering DNA fragments smaller than 100 bp, add an additional 1.5 volumes of anhydrous ethanol.
4. Place the HiPure DNA column into a collection tube. Transfer the mixture to the DNA column and centrifuge at 12,000 × g for 30–60 seconds.
5. Perform the operations in Steps 6–8 of Step 1.
6. Measure the concentration of the purified DNA using a NanoDrop.
