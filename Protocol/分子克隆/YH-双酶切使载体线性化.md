# FastDigest 双酶切线性化质粒载体

## 材料

待线性化质粒 DNA、Thermo Scientific FastDigest 限制性内切酶 I 和 II、10× FastDigest Buffer（或 10× FastDigest Green Buffer）、无核酸酶水、PCR 管。

## 仪器

移液器、微量离心机、恒温金属浴或水浴。

## Protocol

1. 根据质粒序列和目标切割位点选择两种 FastDigest 限制性内切酶，查阅对应酶的说明书，确认反应温度、所需时间及失活条件；若两种酶的最佳反应温度不同，应按照各酶说明书确定分步酶切条件。

2. 按以下体系配制 **20 μL 双酶切反应**（以加入 2 μL、总量不超过 1 μg 的质粒 DNA 为例）：

   | 试剂 | 用量 |
   | --- | ---: |
   | Nuclease-free water | 14 μL |
   | 10× FastDigest Buffer / FastDigest Green Buffer | 2 μL |
   | Plasmid DNA（≤ 1 μg） | 2 μL |
   | FastDigest enzyme I | 1 μL |
   | FastDigest enzyme II | 1 μL |
   | **总体积** | **20 μL** |

3. 轻柔混匀反应体系，短暂离心，使液体集中于管底。

4. 按所用酶的说明书进行恒温孵育。对于均适用于 **37°C** 的 FastDigest 酶，可采用 **37°C、5–15 min** 作为常见快速酶切条件。

5. 酶切完成后，按后续实验要求取少量产物进行琼脂糖凝胶电泳，检查载体是否被线性化。

6. 对需要用于后续连接或组装的线性化载体，按照后续步骤要求回收和纯化 DNA；如需酶失活，应先查阅两种酶各自的说明书。

# Vector Linearization by FastDigest Double Digestion

## Materials

Plasmid DNA to be linearized, Thermo Scientific FastDigest restriction enzymes I and II, 10× FastDigest Buffer (or 10× FastDigest Green Buffer), nuclease-free water, and PCR tubes.

## Equipment

Micropipettes, microcentrifuge, and a temperature-controlled heating block or water bath.

## Protocol

1. Select two FastDigest restriction enzymes according to the plasmid sequence and intended cleavage sites. Consult the respective product manuals to confirm incubation temperatures, digestion times, and inactivation conditions. If the enzymes require different temperatures, determine an appropriate sequential digestion procedure based on their manuals.

2. Prepare a **20 μL double-digestion reaction** as follows (example using 2 μL of plasmid DNA containing no more than 1 μg of DNA):

   | Component | Volume |
   | --- | ---: |
   | Nuclease-free water | 14 μL |
   | 10× FastDigest Buffer / FastDigest Green Buffer | 2 μL |
   | Plasmid DNA (≤ 1 μg) | 2 μL |
   | FastDigest enzyme I | 1 μL |
   | FastDigest enzyme II | 1 μL |
   | **Total volume** | **20 μL** |

3. Mix the reaction gently and briefly centrifuge to collect the liquid at the bottom of the tube.

4. Incubate under the conditions specified in the product manuals. For FastDigest enzymes compatible with **37°C**, **37°C for 5–15 min** is a common rapid-digestion condition.

5. After digestion, analyze a small aliquot by agarose gel electrophoresis to verify vector linearization. 

6. Recover and purify the linearized DNA as required for subsequent ligation or assembly. If enzyme inactivation is necessary, consult the manuals for both enzymes.
