# 琼脂糖凝胶电泳

## **耗材**

- 琼脂糖
- 50× TAE 缓冲液
- 核酸染料
- DNA Marker
- Loading Buffer
- 锥形瓶
- 50 mL 离心管
- 一次性塑料手套
- 移液器及枪头

## **仪器**

- 微波炉
- 电子天平
- 核酸电泳仪及电泳槽
- 制胶模具、胶托盘、梳子
- 凝胶成像系统

## **实验流程**

1. **配胶**：称取 0.25 g 琼脂糖放入锥形瓶，使用 50 mL 离心管新配 25 mL 1× TAE 缓冲液并加入锥形瓶。放入微波炉中高火加热 1 min，沸腾后取出观察；若溶液中还有透明悬浮颗粒，继续加热 10s 直至完全融化。
2. **加入染料**：将融化的琼脂糖溶液室温冷却至 50–60°C，加入 2.5 μL 核酸染料，摇匀。
3. **倒胶**：将胶液倒入架好梳子的制胶模具中，室温静置约半小时至凝固。
4. **安装胶体**：垂直拔出梳子，将胶连同胶托盘一起放入加入 1× TAE 缓冲液的电泳槽中。注意方向，核酸会从负极流向正极。
5. **点样**：点 5 μL DNA Marker。取 5 μL DNA 样品与 1 μL Loading Buffer 混匀后点入点样孔。
6. **电泳**：设定电压 120V，电泳 30 min，可通过胶中溴酚蓝位置判断电泳情况。
7. **凝胶成像**：戴上手套取出凝胶，放入凝胶成像仪中。调整胶位置并关闭舱门，确保胶底部无气泡后点击拍摄。

# Agarose Gel Electrophoresis

## **Materials and Consumables**

- Agarose
- 50× TAE buffer
- Nucleic acid dye
- DNA Marker
- Loading Buffer
- Erlenmeyer flask
- 50 mL centrifuge tube
- Disposable plastic gloves
- Pipettes and tips

## **Instruments**

- Microwave oven
- Electronic balance
- Nucleic acid electrophoresis apparatus and tank
- Gel casting mold, gel tray, and comb
- Gel imaging system

## **Experimental Procedure**

1. **Gel preparation**: Weigh 0.25 g of agarose into an Erlenmeyer flask, prepare 25 mL of 1× TAE buffer using a 50 mL centrifuge tube, and add it to the flask. Heat in a microwave oven on medium-high for 1 min. Remove and check after boiling; if transparent suspended particles remain, continue heating for 10s until completely melted.
2. **Add dye**: Cool the melted agarose solution to 50–60°C at room temperature, add 2.5 μL of nucleic acid dye, and mix well.
3. **Pour the gel**: Pour the gel solution into the gel casting mold with the comb inserted, and leave at room temperature for about half an hour to solidify.
4. **Assemble the gel**: Vertically pull out the comb and place the gel along with the gel tray into the electrophoresis tank filled with 1× TAE buffer. Pay attention to the orientation; nucleic acids migrate from the negative to the positive electrode.
5. **Loading**: Load 5 μL of DNA Marker. Mix 5 μL of DNA sample with 1 μL of Loading Buffer and load into the wells.
6. **Electrophoresis**: Set the voltage to 120V and run for 30 min. The electrophoresis progress can be judged by the position of bromophenol blue in the gel.
7. **Gel imaging**: Wearing gloves, take out the gel and place it into the gel imaging system. Adjust the gel position and close the door. Ensure there are no bubbles at the bottom of the gel before capturing the image.
