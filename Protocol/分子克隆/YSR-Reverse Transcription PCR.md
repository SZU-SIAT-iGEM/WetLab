# 反转录 PCR

## 耗材

- TransScript® All-in-One First-Strand cDNA Synthesis SuperMix for qPCR (AT341)

- 模板 RNA

- RNase-free Water

- 无 RNase 的 PCR 管和枪头

## 仪器

- PCR仪

## 反应流程

1. **配制反应体系（20μL）**
- Total RNA / mRNA: ≤1 μg / ≤100 ng

- 5× TransScript® All-in-One SuperMix for qPCR: 4 μL

- gDNA Remover: 1 μL

- RNase-free Water: 补至 20 μL
2. **反转录反应：**
- 轻轻混匀，短暂离心。在 PCR 仪上运行以下程序：

- 42℃ 孵育 15 分钟（cDNA 合成及 gDNA 去除）

- 85℃ 加热 5 秒（失活 TransScript RT/RI 和 gDNA Remover）

# Reverse Transcription PCR

## Materials

- TransScript® All-in-One First-Strand cDNA Synthesis SuperMix for qPCR (AT341)

- Template RNA

- RNase-free Water

- RNase-free PCR tubes and pipette tips

## Equipment

- PCR Thermocycler

## Procedure

1. **Prepare the Reaction System (20 μL)**
- Total RNA / mRNA: ≤1 μg / ≤100 ng

- 5× TransScript® All-in-One SuperMix for qPCR: 4 μL

- gDNA Remover: 1 μL

- RNase-free Water: add to 20 μL
2. **Reverse Transcription Reaction:**
- Mix gently and briefly centrifuge. Run the following program on a PCR thermocycler:

- 42°C for 15 min (cDNA synthesis and gDNA removal)

- 85°C for 5 sec (Inactivate TransScript RT/RI and gDNA Remover)
