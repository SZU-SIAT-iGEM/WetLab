# 大肠杆菌质粒提取

## **耗材**

胰蛋白胨、NaCl、酵母提取物、HCl、NaOH、DNA抽提液、3 M NaAc溶液、抗生素、诱导剂、大型质粒DNA提取试剂盒（内含RNase、Buffer P1、Buffer P2、Buffer LEN3、Buffer LN4、Buffer PW1、Buffer PW2、Buffer TE、Clear Maxi Syringe、HiPure DNA Maxi Column II、50 mL Collection Tube，美基生物）、无水乙醇、75%乙醇、无菌ddH2O、1.5 mL EP管、50 mL离心管、15 mL离心管、高速离心管、500 mL锥形瓶、灭菌吸头（1 mL、200 μL）、1 L量筒。

## **仪器**

超净工作台、高压蒸汽灭菌锅、-80 ℃冰箱、-20 ℃冰箱、-4 ℃冰箱、恒温摇床、高速冷冻离心机、涡旋振荡器、移液枪、核酸浓度检测仪、恒温干燥箱、微量分光光度计、pH计。

## **实验流程**

1. LB液体培养基的配制

1)按配方称取10 g胰蛋白胨、5 g酵母提取物、10 g NaCl，置于烧杯中（1000 mL体系）。
2)溶解：加入适量ddH2O，搅拌至完全溶解，定容至1000 mL。
3)调pH：用 NaOH或HCl调节pH至7.0～7.4。
4)分装灭菌：分装至培养瓶，封口后121 ℃高压蒸汽灭菌20 min。
5)冷却使用：灭菌后冷却至室温，备用。

2、菌体扩大培养

1)向50 mL无菌离心管中加入20 mL LB液体培养基。
2)从-80 ℃超低温冰箱中取大肠杆菌CA+甘油保藏管，在超净工作台中取菌液并加入培养基中。
3)向培养基中加入1/1000体积的对应抗生素母液。
4)将离心管置于恒温摇床，设置参数为37℃ 、220 rpm ，振荡培养12~18 h, 至菌液达到饱和状态。
5)将前一夜培养好的菌液全部转接入提前灭菌准备好的500 mL LB液体培养基中。
6)在37 ℃ 、220 rpm条件下培养2~4 h。
7)培养结束后，向500 mL菌液中补加1/1000体积的对应抗生素母液。
8)向上述培养液中加入1/200体积的诱导剂。
9)继续在37℃ 、220 rpm条件下振荡培养6~8 h。
10)将诱导完成的菌液倒入50 mL离心管中。在6000 rpm条件下离心10 min。
11)弃去上清液，收集底部的菌体沉淀。所得菌体存放于-20 ℃备用。

3、大肠杆菌质粒提取

1)向菌液中加入10 mL BufferP1/RNase A混合液，涡旋重悬细菌直至观察不到细胞团块（使用前，须确保RNase A已经加到Buffer P1中）。把重悬液转移至合适的高速离心管中。
2)向离心管中加入10 mL Buffer P2，轻柔颠倒混匀8~12次，室温静置2 min，其间偶尔颠倒混匀数次，至溶液清亮。
3)加入5 mL预冷至4°C的Buffer LEN3，立即轻柔颠倒混匀10~15次。5000 rpm离心20 min。
4)取出过滤器(Clear Maxi Syringe)活塞，将上清倒入过滤器中。
5)测量滤液体积，加入等倍体积的Bufer LN4至上清液中。颠倒或涡旋混匀。
6)将HiPure DNA Maxi Column套在50 mL收集管中。转移20 mL上清液至柱子中。
7)5000 rpm离心3 min，倒弃流出液，把柱子套回收集管中。把剩余的上清液转移至柱子中。5000 rpm离心3 min，重复此步至所有的上清液都转移至柱子中离心过滤。
8)倒弃滤液，把柱子套回收集管中。
9)加入5 mL Buffer PW1至柱子中，静置2 min。5000 rpm离心3 min。
10)加入15 mL Bufer PW2(已用无水乙醇稀释)至柱子中，倒弃滤液，把柱子套回收集管中。5000 rpm离心3 min。
11)倒弃滤液，把柱子套回收集管中，5000 rpm离心15 min。
12)取出吸附柱，于50~55°C烘干10 min。
13)把柱子套在灭菌的50 mL离心管中。加入0.8 mL Buffer TE至柱子膜中央，静置3 min。5000 rpm离心3 min。
14)把洗脱液转移至柱子膜中央，静置3 min。5000 rpm离心3 min。

4、磁珠纯化

1)磁珠结合：取质粒提取上清液，加入等体积结合液与20 μL 充分混匀的磁珠悬液，轻柔颠倒混匀10次，室温放置5 min（期间每1 min颠倒混匀1次）；将离心管置于磁力架上静置2 min，待磁珠完全吸附后，小心吸弃上清。
2)磁珠洗涤：取下离心管，加入600 μL 已加无水乙醇的洗涤液，轻柔吹打重悬磁珠；重新置于磁力架上静置1 min，吸弃上清，重复洗涤1次；最后一次洗涤后，开盖室温放置5-10 min，彻底晾干磁珠，去除乙醇残留。离心干燥：12000 rpm离心3 min，弃去上清；再次加入1 mL 75%乙醇冲洗沉淀，轻柔重悬后再次12000 rpm离心3 min。
3)向晾干的磁珠中加入50~100 μL无菌ddH₂O，轻柔吹打重悬磁珠，室温放置2 min；再次置于磁力架上静置2 min，待磁珠完全吸附后，将含纯化质粒的上清转移至新的无菌 1.5 mL EP管中。浓度检测：使用核酸浓度检测仪测定质粒浓度，记录浓度数据。



## Plasmid Extraction from *Escherichia coli*

## **Materials**

Tryptone, NaCl, yeast extract, HCl, NaOH, DNA extraction buffer, 3 M NaAc solution, antibiotics, inducer, large‑scale plasmid DNA extraction kit (containing RNase, Buffer P1, Buffer P2, Buffer LEN3, Buffer LN4, Buffer PW1, Buffer PW2, Buffer TE, Clear Maxi Syringe, HiPure DNA Maxi Column II, 50 mL Collection Tube; Meiji Biotech), absolute ethanol, 75% ethanol, sterile ddH₂O, 1.5 mL EP tubes, 50 mL centrifuge tubes, 15 mL centrifuge tubes, high‑speed centrifuge tubes, 500 mL Erlenmeyer flasks, sterile pipette tips (1 mL, 200 μL), 1 L graduated cylinder.

## **Equipment**

Laminar flow hood, autoclave, −80 °C freezer, −20 °C freezer, 4 °C refrigerator, thermostatic shaker, high‑speed refrigerated centrifuge, vortex mixer, pipettes, nucleic acid detector, thermostatic drying oven, microvolume spectrophotometer, pH meter.

## **Protocol**

1. Preparation of LB Liquid Medium

（1）Weigh 10 g tryptone, 5 g yeast extract, and 10 g NaCl according to the formula and place them in a beaker (1000 mL system).
（2）Dissolution: Add an appropriate amount of ddH₂O, stir until completely dissolved, and adjust the volume to 1000 mL.
（3）pH adjustment: Adjust the pH to 7.0–7.4 with NaOH or HCl.
Aliquoting and sterilization: （4）Dispense into culture flasks, seal, and autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
（5）Cooling for use: Cool to room temperature after sterilization and set aside.

2.Large‑Scale Culture of Bacteria

（1）Add 20 mL LB liquid medium to a 50 mL sterile centrifuge tube.
（2）Take a glycerol stock tube of Escherichia coli CA+ from the −80 °C ultra‑low temperature freezer, and inoculate the bacterial solution into the medium in a laminar flow hood.
（3）Add 1/1000 volume of the corresponding antibiotic stock solution to the medium.
（4）Place the centrifuge tube in a thermostatic shaker, set at 37 °C, 220 rpm, and culture with shaking for 12–18 h until the bacterial solution reaches saturation.
（5）Transfer all the bacterial culture from the previous night into 500 mL pre‑sterilized LB liquid medium.
（6）Incubate at 37 °C, 220 rpm for 2–4 h.
（7）After incubation, add 1/1000 volume of the corresponding antibiotic stock solution to the 500 mL bacterial culture.
（8）Add 1/200 volume of inducer to the above culture.
（9）Continue shaking culture at 37 °C, 220 rpm for 6–8 h.
Pour the induced bacterial culture into 50 mL centrifuge tubes. Centrifuge at 6000 rpm for 10 min.
（10）Discard the supernatant and collect the bacterial pellet at the bottom. Store the obtained bacteria at −20 °C for later use.


3.Plasmid Extraction from Escherichia coli

（1）Add 10 mL Buffer P1/RNase A mixture to the pellet, resuspend thoroughly by vortex until no clumps are visible. Transfer the suspension to a suitable high‑speed centrifuge tube.
（2）Add 10 mL Buffer P2, mix gently by inverting 8–12 times, incubate at room temperature for 2 min, and invert occasionally until the solution becomes clear.
（3）Add 5 mL pre‑chilled (4 °C) Buffer LEN3, mix immediately by gentle inversion 10–15 times, then centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 20 min.
（4）Remove the plunger of the Clear Maxi Syringe filter and pour the supernatant into the filter.
（5）Measure the filtrate volume and add an equal volume of Buffer LN4, then mix by inversion or vortex.
（6）Place the HiPure DNA Maxi Column onto a 50 mL collection tube. Load 20 mL of the supernatant onto the column.
（7）Centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 3 min, discard the flow‑through, and reload the remaining supernatant. Repeat until all solution is processed.
（8）Discard the filtrate and place the column back into the collection tube.
（9）Add 5 mL Buffer PW1 to the column, incubate for 2 min, then centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 3 min.
（10）Add 15 mL Buffer PW2 (diluted with absolute ethanol), discard the flow‑through, and centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 3 min.
（11）Discard the filtrate and centrifuge the column at 5000 rpm for 15 min to remove residual wash buffer.
（12）Remove the column and dry at 50–55 °C for 10 min.
（13）Place the column in a sterile 50 mL centrifuge tube. Add 0.8 mL Buffer TE to the center of the membrane, incubate for 3 min, then centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 3 min.
（14）Reload the eluate onto the column membrane, incubate for 3 min, and centrifuge again at 5000 rpm for 3 min to maximize plasmid yield.


4.Plasmid Purification 

（1）Magnetic Bead Binding
  Take the plasmid eluate, add an equal volume of binding buffer and 20 μL well‑mixed magnetic bead suspension. Mix gently by inverting 10 times and incubate at room temperature for 5 min, inverting once every minute. Place the tube on a magnetic stand for 2 min until beads are fully captured, then carefully discard the supernatant.
（2）Magnetic Bead Washing
Remove the tube from the magnetic stand, add 600 μL wash buffer supplemented with absolute ethanol, and gently resuspend the beads. Place back on the magnetic stand for 1 min and discard the supernatant. Repeat the wash once. After the final wash, air‑dry with the lid open for 5–10 min to eliminate residual ethanol.
For centrifugal drying: centrifuge at 12000 rpm for 3 min and discard supernatant. Add 1 mL 75% ethanol to wash the pellet, resuspend gently, and centrifuge again at 12000 rpm for 3 min.
（3）Elution and Concentration Measurement
Add 50–100 μL sterile ddH₂O to the dried beads, resuspend gently, and incubate at room temperature for 2 min. Place on the magnetic stand for another 2 min, then transfer the supernatant containing purified plasmid to a new sterile 1.5 mL EP tube.
