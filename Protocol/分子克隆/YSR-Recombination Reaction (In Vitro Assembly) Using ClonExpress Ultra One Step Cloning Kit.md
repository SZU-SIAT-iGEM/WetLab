# 使用 ClonExpress 无缝克隆试剂盒构建重组质粒

## 耗材

- ClonExpress Ultra One Step Cloning Kit V3

- 线性化载体

- 插入片段（两端带有 15–20 bp 同源臂的已纯化 DNA）

- ddH₂O

## 仪器

- Nanodrop

- 金属浴

- 移液枪及无菌枪头

## 实验流程

1. **计算 DNA 使用量**
- 单片段重组反应： 最适载体使用量 = [0.02 × 克隆载体碱基对数] ng (0.03 pmol)；最适插入片段使用量 = [0.04 × 插入片段碱基对数] ng (0.06 pmol)。(摩尔比 1:2)

- 多片段重组反应 (n ≤ 10)： 最适载体使用量 = [0.02 × 克隆载体碱基对数] ng (0.03 pmol)；每个片段的最适使用量 = [0.02 × 每个片段碱基对数] ng (0.03 pmol)。(摩尔比 1:1)

- 注：推荐各组分使用量在 10–100 ng 内。当计算值低于或高于此范围时，直接使用最低或最高投入量。

- 使用 Nanodrop 测量载体与插入片段的浓度，以推算出各组分所需体积。
2. **于冰上配制反应体系（总体积 10 μl）**
- 线性化载体：X μl

- 插入片段：Y μl

- 2×CE Mix V3：5 μl

- ddH₂O：补充至 10 μl

- 注：若未纯化的 PCR 产物直接用于重组，加入体积不应超过反应体系总体积的 1/5（即最多 2 μl）。各组分加样量不低于 1 μl，以确保加样准确。
3. **混匀与离心**
- 使用移液器轻轻吸打混匀（切勿振荡混匀）。

- 短暂离心，将反应液收集至管底。
4. **孵育反应**
- 单片段重组： 50℃ 反应 5–15 min。

- 多片段重组： 50℃ 反应 15–30 min（若片段数 >5，建议延长至 30 min）。

- 注：推荐在 PCR 仪等温控比较精确的仪器上进行反应。
5. **冷却与保存**
- 反应结束后，立即降至 4℃ 或置于冰上冷却。

- 重组产物可于 -20℃ 存放一周，待需要时解冻转化。

# Construction of Recombinant Plasmids Using ClonExpress Seamless Cloning Kit

## **Materials**

- ClonExpress Ultra One Step Cloning Kit V3

- Linearized vector

- Insert fragments (purified DNA with 15–20 bp homologous arms at both ends)

- ddH₂O

## **Equipment**

- Nanodrop

- Metal bath (Dry bath incubator)

- Pipettes and sterile tips

## **Procedure**

1. **Calculate DNA Amounts**
- Single-fragment recombination: Optimal vector amount = [0.02 × vector length (bp)] ng (0.03 pmol); Optimal insert amount = [0.04 × insert length (bp)] ng (0.06 pmol). (Molar ratio 1:2)

- Multi-fragment recombination (n ≤ 10): Optimal vector amount = [0.02 × vector length (bp)] ng (0.03 pmol); Optimal amount for each fragment = [0.02 × fragment length (bp)] ng (0.03 pmol). (Molar ratio 1:1)

- *Note: The recommended usage for each component is 10–100 ng. If the calculated value is below or above this range, use the minimum or maximum input amount directly.*

- Measure the concentrations of the vector and insert fragments using a Nanodrop to calculate the required volume for each component.
2. **Prepare the Reaction System on Ice (Total volume 10 μl)**
- Linearized vector: X μl

- Insert fragment(s): Y μl

- 2×CE Mix V3: 5 μl

- ddH₂O: add to 10 μl

- *Note: If unpurified PCR products are used directly for recombination, the added volume should not exceed 1/5 of the total reaction volume (i.e., maximum 2 μl). The volume of each component should not be less than 1 μl to ensure pipetting accuracy.*
3. **Mix and Centrifuge**
- Mix gently by pipetting up and down (do not vortex).

- Briefly centrifuge to collect the reaction mixture at the bottom of the tube.
4. **Incubate the Reaction**
- Single-fragment recombination: Incubate at 50°C for 5–15 min.

- Multi-fragment recombination: Incubate at 50°C for 15–30 min (if the number of fragments is >5, it is recommended to extend the time to 30 min).

- *Note: It is recommended to perform the reaction in a PCR thermocycler or other instrument with precise temperature control.*
5. **Cool and Store**
- After the reaction, immediately cool to 4°C or place on ice.

- The recombination product can be stored at -20°C for up to one week and thawed for transformation when needed.
