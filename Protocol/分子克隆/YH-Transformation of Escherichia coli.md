# 感受态大肠杆菌转化实验

## **耗材**

感受态细胞、质粒或连接混合物体系、一次性涂布器、加入了抗生素的LB固体培养基平板、2M浓度葡萄糖母液、LB液体培养基

## **仪器**

冰盒，金属浴，恒温培养箱

## **实验流程**

1、将感受态细胞在冰上融化

2、配制感受态细胞体系如下，混合后用移液枪轻微搅拌

| 试剂       | 用量 |
| ---------- | ---- |
| 感受态细胞 | 50μl |
| 质粒体系   | 2μl  |

3、30min冰镇，于42℃热激 45s，可用金属浴，但不可震荡。随后再冰镇2min

4、加入500μl的液体LB培养基，额外加入5μl的的2M葡萄糖母液（至终浓度为20 mM），为细菌提供转化的营养，且不可含有任何抗生素。

5、37℃，200rpm震荡防止细菌沉淀，1小时，使转化后的细菌复苏。

6、取100μl复苏后的菌液，涂布在LB选择培养基平板上。

7、使用封口膜封闭培养皿，于恒温培养箱37℃倒置培养过夜。



# Competent E. coli Transformation

## **Materials**

Competent cells、Plasmid or ligation mixture、Disposable spreader、LB solid agar plates supplemented with antibiotics

## **Equipment**

Ice box、Metal bath (or dry bath)、Incubator

## **Protocol**

1. Thaw the competent cells on ice.

2. Prepare the transformation mixture as follows and gently mix with a pipette tip:

   | Reagent                  | Volume |
   | ------------------------ | ------ |
   | Competent cells          | 50 µL  |
   | Plasmid/ligation mixture | 2 µL   |

3. Incubate the mixture on ice for 30 min. Then heat-shock at 42 °C for 45 s (using a metal bath; do not shake). Immediately transfer back to ice for 2 min.

4. Add 500 µL of LB liquid medium containing 20 mM glucose (prepared by adding 1/100 volume of 2 M glucose stock).

5. Incubate at 37 °C with shaking at 200 rpm for 1 hour to allow the transformed bacteria to recover (shaking prevents cell sedimentation).

6. Spread 100 µL of the recovered culture onto an LB selective plate (containing antibiotics).

7. Seal the plate with Parafilm and incubate inverted at 37 °C overnight.
