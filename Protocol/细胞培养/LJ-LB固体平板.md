# LB 固体平板（LB + 1.5% Agar）/ LB Agar Plates

## **耗材**

| 成分 | 用量（per 1 L） | 终浓度 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 胰蛋白胨 Tryptone | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| 酵母提取物 Yeast extract | 5 g | 5 g/L |
| NaCl | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| 琼脂粉 Agar | 15 g | 1.5% (w/v) |
| ddH₂O | 定容至 1 L | — |
| 抗生素母液（可选） | 按热稳定性加入 | — |

## **仪器**

电子天平、加热磁力搅拌器、pH 计、高压蒸汽灭菌锅、超净工作台、90 mm 培养皿

## **实验流程**

1. 称取胰蛋白胨 10 g、酵母提取物 5 g、NaCl 10 g、琼脂粉 15 g，置于烧杯。
2. 加约 950 mL ddH₂O，加热搅拌至琼脂完全溶解。
3. 调 pH 至 7.0–7.4，ddH₂O 定容至 1 L。
4. 121 ℃ 高压灭菌 20 min。
5. 冷却至 50–55 ℃，如需抗性按热稳定性加入抗生素母液，混匀。
6. 无菌倒板（90 mm 每皿约 20–25 mL），凝固后倒置保存。

## Materials

| Component | Amount (per 1 L) | Final |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tryptone | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| Yeast extract | 5 g | 5 g/L |
| NaCl | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| Agar | 15 g | 1.5% (w/v) |
| ddH₂O | to 1 L | — |
| Antibiotic stock (optional) | per heat stability | — |

## Equipment

Analytical balance, heated magnetic stirrer, pH meter, autoclave, clean bench, 90 mm petri dishes

## Protocol

1. Weigh 10 g tryptone, 5 g yeast extract, 10 g NaCl and 15 g agar into a beaker.
2. Add ~950 mL ddH₂O and heat with stirring until the agar dissolves completely.
3. Adjust pH to 7.0–7.4; make to 1 L with ddH₂O.
4. Autoclave at 121 ℃ for 20 min.
5. Cool to 50–55 ℃; add antibiotic stock if required (per heat stability) and mix.
6. Pour ~20–25 mL per 90 mm plate aseptically; store inverted after solidifying.
