# 甘油保存大肠杆菌细胞
---
## 耗材
---
大肠杆菌细胞、LB液体培养基、甘油、去离子水、液氮、注射器、无菌0.22 µm孔径针头式过滤器、无菌微量离心管（EP 管）、15${mL}$锥形离心管
## 仪器
---
移液枪、超净工作台

## 实验流程
---
1. 将纯甘油与去离子水以1：1体积混合，配置成50%的甘油储液，使用无菌0.22 µm孔径针头式过滤器进行过滤除菌
2. 将菌液与50%甘油等体积混合，至甘油终浓度为25%
3. 将处理好的样本放入液氮速冻，完成后放入-80${℃}$ 的冰箱中保存

# Glycerol-preserved Escherichia coli cells
---
## Consumables
---
_Escherichia coli_ cells, LB liquid medium, glycerol, deionized water, liquid nitrogen, syringes, sterile 0.22 μm pore-size syringe filters, sterile microcentrifuge tubes (EP tubes), 15 mL conical centrifuge tubes
## Equipment
---
Micropipettes, laminar flow hood (clean bench)
## Experimental Procedure
---
1. Mix pure glycerol with deionized water at a 1:1 volume ratio to prepare a 50% glycerol stock solution. Sterilize the solution via filtration using a sterile 0.22 μm pore-size syringe filter.
2. Combine the bacterial culture with the 50% glycerol stock solution at an equal volume ratio to achieve a final glycerol concentration of 25%.
3. Flash-freeze the prepared samples in liquid nitrogen, then transfer and store them in a -80 ℃ freezer.
