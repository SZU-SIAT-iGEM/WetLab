# M9培养基配制及无碳源1×M9洗涤重悬

## **耗材**

- 5×M9基础盐干粉
- 1 M 硫酸镁
- 1 M 氯化钙
- 纤维二糖或葡萄糖
- 超纯水
- 0.22 μm 滤膜及注射器
- 无菌离心管、玻璃广口瓶
- 移液器及枪头

## **仪器**

- 磁力搅拌器
- 高压灭菌锅
- 超净台
- 离心机
- 分光光度计或酶标仪

## **实验流程**

1. **5×M9基础盐母液配制**
   2. 称取 56.4 g 5×M9 基础盐干粉，加入部分超纯水，使用磁力搅拌器充分搅拌至完全溶解。
   3. 用超纯水补足体积至 1 L。
   4. 将溶液进行高压灭菌，灭菌后即得 5×M9 培养基基盐。

5. **无碳源1×M9工作液配制**
   6. 在超净台中，按每升加入 200 mL 无菌 5×M9 母液的比例进行配制。
   7. 加入无菌的 1 mL 1 M 硫酸镁和 0.1 mL 1 M 氯化钙。
   8. 用无菌超纯水补足剩余体积至 1 L，混匀。

9. **含碳源1×M9培养基配制**
   10. 现配纤维二糖或葡萄糖溶液，使用 0.22 μm 滤膜过滤除菌。
   11. 将过滤除菌后的碳源加入至无碳源1×M9工作液中，混匀。

12. **菌体收集与无碳源洗涤**
   13. 将菌株培养至对数期。
   14. 离心收集菌体。
   15. 弃去上清，用不含碳源的1×M9工作液洗涤菌体 2–3 次。

16. **重悬与浓度调整**
   17. 最后一次洗涤离心后，弃去上清。
   18. 使用不含碳源的1×M9工作液重悬菌体。
   19. 测定菌液 \(OD_{600}\)，将菌体浓度调至实验计划使用的 \(OD_{600}=0.1\)。

# M9 Medium Preparation and Carbon-Free 1× M9 Washing and Resuspension

## **Materials and Consumables**

- 5× M9 basal salt powder
- 1 M Magnesium sulfate
- 1 M Calcium chloride
- Cellobiose or glucose
- Ultrapure water
- 0.22 μm filter membrane and syringe
- Sterile centrifuge tubes, glass wide-mouth bottles
- Pipettes and tips

## **Instruments**

- Magnetic stirrer
- Autoclave
- Clean bench
- Centrifuge
- Spectrophotometer or microplate reader

## **Experimental Procedure**

1. **Preparation of 5× M9 Basal Salt Stock Solution**
   2. Weigh 56.4 g of 5× M9 basal salt powder, add a portion of ultrapure water, and stir thoroughly using a magnetic stirrer until completely dissolved.
   3. Make up the volume to 1 L with ultrapure water.
   4. Autoclave the solution for sterilization to obtain the 5× M9 basal salt stock solution.

5. **Preparation of Carbon-Free 1× M9 Working Solution**
   6. In a clean bench, prepare the solution at a ratio of 200 mL of sterile 5× M9 stock solution per liter.
   7. Add 1 mL of sterile 1 M magnesium sulfate and 0.1 mL of 1 M calcium chloride.
   8. Make up the remaining volume to 1 L with sterile ultrapure water and mix well. 

9. **Preparation of 1× M9 Medium with Carbon Source**
   10. Prepare cellobiose or glucose solution fresh and filter-sterilize using a 0.22 μm filter membrane.
   11. Add the filter-sterilized carbon source to the carbon-free 1× M9 working solution and mix well.

12. **Cell Harvesting and Carbon-Free Washing**
   13. Cultivate the strain to the logarithmic growth phase.
   14. Collect the cells by centrifugation.
   15. Discard the supernatant and wash the cells 2–3 times with the carbon-free 1× M9 working solution.

16. **Resuspension and Concentration Adjustment**
   17. After the final wash and centrifugation, discard the supernatant.
   18. Resuspend the cells using the carbon-free 1× M9 working solution.
   19. Measure the \(OD_{600}\) and adjust the cell concentration to the planned experimental \(OD_{600}=0.1\).
