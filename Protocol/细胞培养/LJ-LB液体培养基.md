# LB 液体培养基 / LB Liquid Medium

## **耗材**

| 成分 | 用量（per 1 L） | 终浓度 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 胰蛋白胨 Tryptone | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| 酵母提取物 Yeast extract | 5 g | 5 g/L |
| NaCl | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| ddH₂O | 定容至 1 L | — |
| NaOH / HCl | 调 pH 用 | — |

## **仪器**

电子天平、磁力搅拌器、pH 计、量筒或容量瓶（1 L）、高压蒸汽灭菌锅、培养瓶

## **实验流程**

1. 称取胰蛋白胨 10 g、酵母提取物 5 g、NaCl 10 g，置于烧杯中。
2. 加入约 950 mL ddH₂O，磁力搅拌至完全溶解。
3. 用 NaOH 或 HCl 调 pH 至 7.0–7.4，ddH₂O 定容至 1 L。
4. 分装至培养瓶，封口。
5. 121 ℃ 高压灭菌 20 min，冷却后使用。

## Materials

| Component | Amount (per 1 L) | Final |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tryptone | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| Yeast extract | 5 g | 5 g/L |
| NaCl | 10 g | 10 g/L |
| ddH₂O | to 1 L | — |
| NaOH / HCl | for pH adjustment | — |

## Equipment

Analytical balance, magnetic stirrer, pH meter, graduated cylinder or 1 L volumetric flask, autoclave, culture flasks

## Protocol

1. Weigh 10 g tryptone, 5 g yeast extract and 10 g NaCl into a beaker.
2. Add ~950 mL ddH₂O and stir until fully dissolved.
3. Adjust pH to 7.0–7.4 with NaOH or HCl; make to 1 L with ddH₂O.
4. Dispense into culture flasks and seal.
5. Autoclave at 121 ℃ for 20 min; cool before use.
