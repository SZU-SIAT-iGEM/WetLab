# 纤维二糖溶液（100 mg/mL 母液）/ Cellobiose Stock (100 mg/mL)

## **耗材**

| 成分 | 用量（per 100 mL） |
| --- | --- |
| 纤维二糖 | 10 g |
| ddH₂O | 定容至 100 mL |

## **仪器**

电子天平、量筒或容量瓶（100 mL）、磁力搅拌器（可选）、0.22 μm 滤膜与注射器、无菌容器

## **实验流程**

1. 称取纤维二糖 10 g。
2. 加约 70 mL ddH₂O 搅拌至完全溶解，定容至 100 mL（即 100 mg/mL 母液）。
3. 用 0.22 μm 滤膜过滤除菌（不可高压灭菌）。
4. 分装至无菌容器，4 ℃ 保存。
5. 使用时按需稀释：如配 M9 碳源终浓度 0.2%（w/v）= 2 mg/mL，取母液稀释 50 倍；DNS 法检测体系按需稀释至目标浓度。

---

## Materials

| Component | Amount (per 100 mL) |
| --- | --- |
| Cellobiose | 10 g |
| ddH₂O | to 100 mL |

## Equipment

Analytical balance, 100 mL graduated cylinder or volumetric flask, magnetic stirrer (optional), 0.22 μm filter and syringe, sterile container

## Protocol

1. Weigh 10 g cellobiose.
2. Dissolve in ~70 mL ddH₂O with stirring; make to 100 mL (100 mg/mL stock).
3. Filter-sterilise through 0.22 μm (do not autoclave).
4. Dispense into sterile containers and store at 4 ℃.
5. Dilute before use: for a 0.2% (w/v) = 2 mg/mL M9 carbon source, dilute the stock 50-fold; for the DNS assay, dilute to the target concentration as needed.
