纤维二糖代谢功能验证（生长曲线测定）

**实验目的：**
验证 *ascF* 和 *ascB* 基因在MG1655中表达后，能否赋予菌株利用纤维二糖作为唯一碳源生长的能力。

**实验耗材：**
*   **培养基：** M9最低盐培养基（共1L）：磷酸氢二钠6.78g，磷酸氢二钾3g，氯化钠0.5g，氯化铵1g，七水合硫酸镁0.12g，氯化钙0.011g， 纤维二糖5g, 葡萄糖5g。
*   **菌株与质粒：**
    *   实验组：MG1655 (pGEX-ascFB)
    *   阳性对照：MG1655 (pGEX-ascFB) + 葡萄糖
    *   阴性对照：MG1655 (pGEX-Empty) + 纤维二糖
    *   空白对照：无菌培养基
*   **耗材：** 96孔板, 枪头, 封口膜。

**实验仪器：**
*   恒温摇床
*   酶标仪（用于测 $OD_{600}$）或分光光度计

**实验流程：**

**步骤一：培养基配制**
1.  **M9基础培养基：** 配制M9最低盐培养基。
	1.配置时先加 **Na₂HPO₄** 再加 **KH₂PO₄**，最后加 **NaCl** 和 **NH₄Cl**。 使用磁力搅拌器充分搅匀，必要时轻度加热（<60℃）帮助溶解
	2.高压灭菌
	3.待冷却后在超净台加入灭菌的1ml 1M MgSO₄，0.1ml 1M CaCl₂
	4.现配纤维二糖溶液，过滤灭菌，加入
	5.不足1L部分使用超纯水补足
2.  **添加碳源：**
    *   **实验组培养基：** M9 + 纤维二糖。（工程菌）
    *   **阳性对照培养基：** M9 +  葡萄糖。（工程菌）
    *   **阴性对照培养基：** M9 + 纤维二糖。（野生菌）
    *   **空白对照：** 无菌 M9 + 纤维二糖（不接种细菌）。

**步骤二：菌株接种**
1. 各菌株培养至对数期；
2. 离心收集菌体；
3. 用不含碳源的 chemotaxis buffer 或 motility buffer 洗涤 2–3 次；
4. 重悬并调至实验计划使用的 OD600=0.1；
5. 立即进行梯度稀释涂板（1:100）；

**步骤三：生长曲线测定**
1.  **培养条件：** 将接种好的平板或摇瓶放入恒温摇床，在37°C震荡培养。
2.  **数据采集：** 每隔一定时间（每2小时或4小时），使用酶标仪测定 $OD_{600}$。
3.  **记录：** 记录数据直至生长进入稳定期（通常12-24小时）。

**步骤五：稀释涂布平板测定活细胞浓度**
同步od600的采样，将m9液体培养基的菌经过梯度稀释后涂布,计算单菌落数量，计算活菌浓度

**步骤六：数据分析与预期结果**
1.  绘制生长曲线，Time vs $OD_{600}$，Time vs CFU。
2.  **预期结果：**
    *   **实验组（pGEX-ascFB + 纤维二糖）：** $OD_{600}$ 随时间显著上升，说明菌株能利用纤维二糖生长。
    *   **阳性对照（pGEX-ascFB + 葡萄糖）：** 正常快速生长（验证菌株本身活力及质粒无毒性）。
    *   **阴性对照（空载体 + 纤维二糖）：** $OD_{600}$ 基本不增长或增长极慢（说明MG1655本身不能利用纤维二糖）。
    *   **空白对照：** $OD_{600}$ 无变化（排除培养基污染或沉淀干扰）。

   3. 将od600的测定值与cfu计算值进行对应，两条生长曲线对应，之后仅测定od600即可得到准确的cuf的值


**Experimental Objective:**
To verify whether the expression of the *ascF* and *ascB* genes in MG1655 can endow the strain with the ability to grow using cellobiose as the sole carbon source.

**Experimental Materials and Reagents:**
*   **Media:** M9 minimal salt medium (total 1 L): 6.78 g disodium hydrogen phosphate, 3 g dipotassium hydrogen phosphate, 0.5 g sodium chloride, 1 g ammonium chloride, 0.12 g magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, 0.011 g calcium chloride, 5 g cellobiose, 5 g glucose.
*   **Strains and Plasmids:**
    *   Experimental group: MG1655 (pGEX-ascFB)
    *   Positive control: MG1655 (pGEX-ascFB) + glucose
    *   Negative control: MG1655 (pGEX-Empty) + cellobiose
    *   Blank control: Sterile medium
*   **Consumables:** 96-well plates, pipette tips, sealing film.

**Experimental Instruments:**
*   Constant temperature shaker
*   Microplate reader (for measuring $OD_{600}$) or spectrophotometer

**Experimental Procedure:**

**Step 1: Media Preparation**
1.  **M9 Basal Medium:** Prepare M9 minimal salt medium.
	1. When preparing, add **Na₂HPO₄** first, then **KH₂PO₄**, and finally **NaCl** and **NH₄Cl**. Mix thoroughly using a magnetic stirrer, and heat gently (<60°C) if necessary to aid dissolution.
	2. Autoclave for sterilization.
	3. After cooling, add 1 mL of sterile 1 M MgSO₄ and 0.1 mL of 1 M CaCl₂ in a clean bench.
	4. Prepare the cellobiose solution fresh, filter-sterilize it, and add it to the medium.
	5. Make up the volume to 1 L with ultrapure water.
2.  **Carbon Source Addition:**
    *   **Experimental group medium:** M9 + cellobiose. (Engineered strain)
    *   **Positive control medium:** M9 + glucose. (Engineered strain)
    *   **Negative control medium:** M9 + cellobiose. (Wild-type strain)
    *   **Blank control:** Sterile M9 + cellobiose (no bacteria inoculated).

**Step 2: Strain Inoculation**
1. Cultivate each strain to the logarithmic growth phase;
2. Collect the cells by centrifugation;
3. Wash the cells 2–3 times with carbon-free chemotaxis buffer or motility buffer;
4. Resuspend the cells and adjust the concentration to the planned experimental OD600 of 0.1;
5. Immediately perform serial dilution and plate spreading (1:100);

**Step 3: Growth Curve Measurement**
1.  **Culture Conditions:** Place the inoculated plates or flasks into a constant temperature shaker and cultivate at 37°C with shaking.
2.  **Data Collection:** At regular intervals (every 2 or 4 hours), measure the $OD_{600}$ using a microplate reader.
3.  **Recording:** Record the data until the growth reaches the stationary phase (usually 12–24 hours).

**Step 5: Viable Cell Concentration Measurement by Dilution Plating**
Synchronize with the OD600 sampling; perform serial dilution of the bacteria from the M9 liquid medium and plate them. Calculate the number of single colonies to determine the viable cell concentration.

**Step 6: Data Analysis and Expected Results**
1.  Plot the growth curves: Time vs. $OD_{600}$, and Time vs. CFU.
2.  **Expected Results:**
    *   **Experimental group (pGEX-ascFB + cellobiose):** The $OD_{600}$ increases significantly over time, indicating that the strain can utilize cellobiose for growth.
    *   **Positive control (pGEX-ascFB + glucose):** Normal and rapid growth (to verify the intrinsic vitality of the strain and the non-toxicity of the plasmid).
    *   **Negative control (empty vector + cellobiose):** The $OD_{600}$ shows little to no increase or increases extremely slowly (indicating that MG1655 itself cannot utilize cellobiose).
    *   **Blank control:** No change in $OD_{600}$ (to rule out interference from medium contamination or precipitation).

   3. Correlate the measured OD600 values with the calculated CFU values to align the two growth curves. Thereafter, the accurate CFU value can be obtained by measuring only the OD600.
