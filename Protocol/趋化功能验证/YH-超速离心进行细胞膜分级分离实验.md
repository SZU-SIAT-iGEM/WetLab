# 超速离心细胞分级分离实验

## **耗材**

ascFB工程菌及 EV 空载体对照菌、液体培养基、裂解缓冲液、PMSF、预冷洗涤缓冲液、离心管、与转头兼容的超速离心管、冰、蛋白样品收集管。

## **仪器**

恒温振荡培养箱、常规冷冻离心机、超声细胞破碎仪、冰盒、超速离心机及配套转头、移液器。

## **实验流程**

1、分别培养 ascFB 工程菌和 EV 空载体对照菌，采用 200 mL 锥形瓶培养体系，每瓶接种 2 mL 过夜培养菌液。

2、离心收集两组菌体，弃培养上清。收菌转速、温度及时间在 DBTL 中未记录，需补充原始条件。

3、用裂解缓冲液重悬菌体，在细胞破碎前加入 PMSF ( Phenylmethanesulfonyl fluoride ) 使终浓度为1 mM（原液浓度为0.1M in ethanol）。

裂解缓冲液

| 成分                 | 终浓度             |
| -------------------- | ------------------ |
| Tris-HCl             | 50 mM , pH 7.5–8.0 |
| NaCl                 | 150 mM             |
| Glycerol             | 10%                |
| PMSF(细胞破碎前加入) | 1 mM               |

4、将样品置于冰上进行超声破碎，期间保持低温。超声功率、工作/间歇时间和总破碎时间需根据实际仪器设置补充。

5、取一部分裂解样品作为全细胞裂解液（Whole-cell lysate）留样，用于后续 SDS-PAGE 对照。记录留样体积。

6、将剩余裂解液以 5,000 × g、4°C 离心 10 min，去除未破碎细胞及较大碎片，保留上清。

7、将所得上清转入合适的超速离心管，配平到0.0001g，进行第一次超速离心：180,000 × g、4°C、90 min。

8、第一次超速离心后，小心收集上清，标记为可溶性蛋白组分（Soluble fraction）；保留沉淀，标记为粗膜组分（Crude membrane pellet）。此处的可溶性上清不应直接视为经严格纯化的胞质组分。

9、用 4°C 预冷的洗涤缓冲液充分重悬粗膜沉淀，减少夹带的可溶性蛋白。洗涤液组成和重悬体积需补充原始记录。

洗涤缓冲液

| 成分           | 终浓度             |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| Tris-HCl       | 50 mM , pH 7.5–8.0 |
| NaCl           | 150 mM             |
| PMSF(现配现用) | 1 mM               |

10、将重悬后的粗膜样品进行第二次超速离心。DBTL 记录了再次超速离心，但未明确第二次的离心力和时间，需核对设备运行记录后填写，不能直接认定与第一次相同。

11、弃去第二次超速离心所得上清，保留最终膜沉淀，并用裂解缓冲液重新悬浮，标记为洗涤后的粗膜组分（Washed crude membrane fraction）。

12、分别收集两株菌的全细胞裂解液、可溶性胞质上清组分和洗涤后的粗膜组分用于后续SDS-page。

# Ultracentrifugation-Based Cell Fractionation

## **Materials**

ascFB-engineered strain and empty-vector (EV) control strain, liquid culture medium, lysis buffer, PMSF, prechilled wash buffer, centrifuge tubes, ultracentrifuge tubes compatible with the rotor, ice, and protein sample collection tubes.

## **Equipment**

Temperature-controlled shaking incubator, refrigerated centrifuge, ultrasonic cell disruptor, ice bucket, ultracentrifuge with compatible rotor, and pipettes.

## **Protocol**

1. Culture the ascFB-engineered strain and EV control strain separately using a 200 mL Erlenmeyer flask culture setup. Inoculate each flask with 2 mL of overnight bacterial culture.

2. Collect the cells from both groups by centrifugation and discard the culture supernatant. The centrifugation speed, temperature, and duration for cell harvesting were not recorded in the DBTL and should be added from the original experimental records.

3. Resuspend the cells in lysis buffer. Before cell disruption, add PMSF (phenylmethanesulfonyl fluoride) to a final concentration of 1 mM, using a 0.1 M stock solution prepared in ethanol.

Lysis buffer

| Component | Final concentration |
| --- | --- |
| Tris-HCl | 50 mM, pH 7.5–8.0 |
| NaCl | 150 mM |
| Glycerol | 10% |
| PMSF (add before cell disruption) | 1 mM |

4. Sonicate the samples on ice, maintaining a low temperature throughout disruption. The sonication power, on/off pulse durations, and total disruption time should be added according to the actual instrument settings.

5. Reserve an aliquot of the disrupted sample as the whole-cell lysate for subsequent SDS-PAGE comparison. Record the volume of the reserved aliquot.

6. Centrifuge the remaining lysate at 5,000 × g and 4°C for 10 min to remove intact cells and large cell debris. Retain the supernatant.

7. Transfer the resulting supernatant into suitable ultracentrifuge tubes and balance the tubes to within 0.0001 g. Perform the first ultracentrifugation at 180,000 × g and 4°C for 90 min.

8. After the first ultracentrifugation, carefully collect the supernatant and label it the soluble protein fraction (Soluble fraction). Retain the pellet and label it the crude membrane fraction (Crude membrane pellet). The soluble supernatant should not be regarded as a rigorously purified cytoplasmic fraction.

9. Thoroughly resuspend the crude membrane pellet in wash buffer prechilled to 4°C to reduce the carryover of soluble proteins. The wash volume should be added from the original experimental records.

Wash buffer

| Component | Final concentration |
| --- | --- |
| Tris-HCl | 50 mM, pH 7.5–8.0 |
| NaCl | 150 mM |
| PMSF (prepare fresh before use) | 1 mM |

10. Perform a second ultracentrifugation on the resuspended crude membrane sample. Although the DBTL records this additional ultracentrifugation, it does not specify the centrifugal force or duration. These parameters should be checked against the instrument log rather than assumed to be identical to the first run.
11. Discard the supernatant from the second ultracentrifugation, retain the final membrane pellet, and resuspend it in lysis buffer. Label this sample the washed crude membrane fraction (Washed crude membrane fraction).
12. Collect the whole-cell lysate, soluble supernatant fraction, and washed crude membrane fraction from each of the two strains for SDS-page
