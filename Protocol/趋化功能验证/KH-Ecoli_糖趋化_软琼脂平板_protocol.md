# 软琼脂平板验证细菌趋化能力

## **耗材**

- **软琼脂平板配方（每 1 L）：** Bacto tryptone 10 g（1%）、NaCl 5 g（0.5%）、Bacto agar 3.5 g（0.35%）；纤维二糖/葡萄糖 10 mM，高压冷却至约 50 °C 后无菌加入。
- **平板规格：** 每 100 mm 培养皿倒入 20–25 mL 软琼脂培养基。
- **菌株：** MG1655/工程菌。
- **细菌培养基：** LB 或 1% tryptone broth。
- **其他耗材：** 100 mm 培养皿、2.5 µL 移液器及枪头、直尺/卡尺；拍照定量时可备相机和 ImageJ 软件。

## **仪器**

- 恒温培养箱（30–32 °C，正置培养）
- 恒温摇床（30 °C，200 rpm）
- 高压灭菌锅
- 超净台
- 移液器
- 直尺/卡尺；相机和 ImageJ（可选，用于定时拍照定量环半径）

## **实验流程**

1. **铺板：** 糖过滤除菌；含盐和琼脂的基础培养基高压灭菌后冷却至约 50 °C，在无菌条件下加入糖并混匀；每 100 mm 培养皿倒入 20–25 mL，水平静置凝固至少 1 h。
2. **细菌培养：** 单克隆 → 过夜培养 → 按 1:100 转接 → 30 °C、200 rpm 培养至晚对数期（\(OD_{600}=0.8–1.3\)）；点种前用同种新鲜培养基稀释至 \(OD_{600}\approx0.1\)（约 \(10^8\) CFU/mL）。
3. **接种：** 用 2.5 µL 移液器吸取不超过 1 µL 稀释菌液，将枪头轻插入平板中心琼脂内约 2–3 mm，缓缓打出菌液。
4. **吸收：** 室温静置约 30–60 min，使菌液被琼脂吸收。
5. **培养：** 平板正置，30–32 °C 培养。
6. **观察与测量：** 约 24 h（30 °C）后测量迁移直径；用直尺或卡尺测量环直径（mm），每个处理至少 3 个平行板；也可定时拍照并用 ImageJ 定量环半径随时间变化。
7. **判读：** 外加吸引剂浓度越高，迁移速度越慢；因此与空白对照相比趋化圈更小，说明添加物质对细菌具有趋化吸引作用。

# Verification of Bacterial Chemotaxis Ability Using Soft Agar Plates

## **Materials and Consumables**

- **Soft agar plate formulation (per 1 L):** Bacto tryptone 10 g (1%), NaCl 5 g (0.5%), Bacto agar 3.5 g (0.35%); cellobiose/glucose 10 mM, added aseptically after autoclaving and cooling to approximately 50 °C.
- **Plate specifications:** Pour 20–25 mL of soft agar medium into each 100 mm Petri dish.
- **Strain:** MG1655/engineered strain.
- **Bacterial culture medium:** LB or 1% tryptone broth.
- **Other consumables:** 100 mm Petri dishes, 2.5 µL pipette and tips, ruler/caliper; camera and ImageJ software optional for time-lapse photography and quantification.

## **Instruments**

- Constant-temperature incubator (30–32 °C, upright incubation)
- Constant-temperature shaker (30 °C, 200 rpm)
- Autoclave
- Clean bench
- Pipette
- Ruler/caliper; camera and ImageJ (optional, for time-lapse photography and quantification of ring radius)

## **Experimental Procedure**

1. **Plate preparation:** Filter-sterilize the sugar; autoclave the basal medium containing salts and agar, cool to approximately 50 °C, add the sugar aseptically, and mix well; pour 20–25 mL into each 100 mm Petri dish and leave horizontally to solidify for at least 1 h.
2. **Bacterial culture:** Single colony → overnight culture → transfer at 1:100 → culture at 30 °C and 200 rpm to late logarithmic phase (\(OD_{600}=0.8–1.3\)); before inoculation, dilute with the same fresh medium to \(OD_{600}\approx0.1\) (approximately \(10^8\) CFU/mL).
3. **Inoculation:** Use a 2.5 µL pipette to take no more than 1 µL of diluted bacterial suspension; gently insert the pipette tip about 2–3 mm into the agar at the center of the plate and slowly dispense the bacterial suspension.
4. **Absorption:** Leave at room temperature for approximately 30–60 min to allow the bacterial suspension to be absorbed by the agar.
5. **Incubation:** Incubate the plates upright at 30–32 °C.
6. **Observation and measurement:** Measure the migration diameter after approximately 24 h (at 30 °C); measure the ring diameter (mm) with a ruler or caliper, with at least 3 parallel plates per treatment; alternatively, take timed photographs and use ImageJ to quantify the ring radius over time.
7. **Interpretation:** Higher concentrations of an added attractant slow migration; therefore, a smaller chemotactic ring compared with the blank control indicates that the added substance is a chemoattractant for the bacteria.
