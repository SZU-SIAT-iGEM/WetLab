# SDS-PAGE 蛋白电泳实验

## **耗材**

蛋白样品、SDS-PAGE Loading Buffer、SDS-PAGE 预制胶、蛋白分子量标准（Protein Marker）、电泳缓冲液、考马斯亮蓝染色液、染色容器、离心管、移液枪头。

## **仪器**

沸水浴锅或金属浴、电泳槽及电泳电源、GenScript 凝胶染色脱色仪、凝胶成像设备、移液器。

## **实验流程**

1、将蛋白样品与 SDS-PAGE Loading Buffer 按 4:1 的体积比混匀，重悬。

2、将混合后的样品置于沸水浴或金属浴中加热 3 min，取出后冷却。

3、将预制胶安装至电泳槽，加入电泳缓冲液；每孔上样 15–20 μL 蛋白样品，并在 Marker 孔加入 5 μL Protein Marker。

4、设置电泳电压为 160 V，电泳 30 min。

5、电泳结束后，取出预制胶并放入染色容器中，加入考马斯亮蓝染色液，置于 GenScript 凝胶染色脱色仪中，选择预设程序完成染色与脱色。程序结束后取出凝胶，观察并拍摄蛋白条带。

# SDS-PAGE Protein Electrophoresis

## **Materials**

Protein samples, SDS-PAGE loading buffer, precast SDS-PAGE gels, protein molecular weight marker (Protein Marker), electrophoresis running buffer, Coomassie Brilliant Blue staining solution, staining container, microcentrifuge tubes, and pipette tips.

## **Equipment**

Boiling water bath or dry bath, electrophoresis tank and power supply, GenScript gel staining/destaining instrument, gel imaging system, and pipettes.

## **Protocol**

1. Mix the protein sample with SDS-PAGE loading buffer at a volume ratio of 4:1 and resuspend thoroughly.

2. Heat the mixture in a boiling water bath or dry bath for 3 min, then remove it and allow it to cool.

3. Install the precast gel in the electrophoresis tank and add running buffer. Load 15–20 μL of each protein sample per well and 5 μL of Protein Marker into a separate marker well.

4. Set the electrophoresis voltage to 160 V and run for 30 min.

5. After electrophoresis, remove the precast gel and transfer it to a staining container. Add Coomassie Brilliant Blue staining solution and place the gel in the GenScript gel staining/destaining instrument. Select the preset program to complete staining and destaining. After the program finishes, remove the gel, observe the protein bands, and capture an image.
