# CP 系列 chb 启动子工程菌生长曲线表征

# 甘油菌恢复

## **耗材**

各菌株甘油菌、LB 固体培养基

## **仪器**

37°C 培养箱、接种环或无菌枪头

## **实验流程**

1、从 -80°C 甘油菌中分别取少量菌液，在 LB 平板上划线。

2、WT、CP6CHB、CP7CHB、CP12CHB、CP25CHB、CP30CHB 分别独立划板。

3、37°C 倒置培养 **12–16 h**。

4、每个菌株挑取 **3 个独立单克隆**，作为 3 个生物学重复的起点。

# M9 + glycerol 预培养

## **耗材**

M9 基础培养基、glycerol

## **仪器**

37°C 摇床、分光光度计

## **实验流程**

1、配制 **M9 + 0.2% glycerol**。

2、从甘油复苏菌株平板挑取 **3 个独立单克隆**。

3、将单克隆分别接种到 M9 + 0.2% glycerol。

4、37°C、**200–250 rpm** 振荡培养。

5、培养至 OD600 约 **0.2–0.4**。

6、立即进入收菌和洗涤步骤。

> glycerol 用作统一预培养碳源。它不是 PTS 糖，进入中心代谢时不直接通过葡萄糖 PTS 系统，也较少引入由 glucose PTS 和强碳源阻遏带来的额外生理状态差异。正式实验研究的是 chb PTS 与 cellobiose 利用，因此预培养使用 glycerol 更适合保持各菌株在进入测试前的共同背景。

# 收菌、洗涤与统一起始细胞浓度

## **耗材**

无碳源 M9 基础培养基、离心管

## **仪器**

台式离心机、分光光度计

## **实验流程**

1、将 OD600 约 0.2–0.4 的菌液转入离心管。

2、**4,000–6,000 × g，5–10 min** 离心收菌。

3、弃去上清。

4、用无碳源 M9 重悬。

5、再次离心。

6、重复洗涤 **2 次**。

7、最后用少量（~500ul左右）无碳源 M9 重悬。

8、将重悬各菌株菌液稀释10倍测 OD600，测得的OD600浓度*10为真实OD600浓度。

9、将测得真实OD600的重悬菌液接种于96孔板内的200ul培养基，使接种后各孔最终 OD600 约为 0.1左右。（真实OD600 * 接种菌液体积=最终OD600 * 200ul）

# 正式培养基与实验组设置

## **耗材**

M9、cellobiose、glucose、洗涤后的菌体

## **仪器**

无特殊额外仪器

## **实验流程**

1、配制：

   - **M9 + 0.4% cellobiose**
   - **M9 + 0.4% glucose**

2、设置 cellobiose 主实验组：

   - WT MG1655
   - CP6CHB
   - CP7CHB
   - CP12CHB
   - CP25CHB
   - CP30CHB
   - CP44CHB

3、设置 glucose 对照组：

   - WT MG1655
   - CP6CHB
   - CP7CHB
   - CP12CHB
   - CP25CHB
   - CP30CHB
   - CP44CHB

4、设置无细胞 blank：

   - M9 + 0.4% cellobiose
   - M9 + 0.4% glucose

5、所有组使用同一批 M9 和同一批糖溶液。

# 96 孔板设置

## **耗材**

透明平底 96 孔板、无菌水或 PBS

## **仪器**

移液器

## **实验流程**

1、每孔总体积 **200 μL**。

2、每个生物学重复设置 2-3个技术重复孔。

3、cellobiose blank 与 glucose blank 各设置 3 个孔。

4、正式实验孔放在板内区域。

5、外围未使用孔加入 **200 μL 无菌水或 PBS**，降低蒸发和边缘效应。

6、不同实验组在板内位置均衡分布。

7、上板前保存 plate map。

# 生长曲线测量

## **耗材**

已接种的 96 孔板

## **仪器**

带恒温和振荡功能的酶标仪

## **实验流程**

1、上板后立即测量 OD600，记为 **t = 0 h**。

2、温度设为 **37°C**。

3、每次读数前进行振荡。

4、连续监测 **24 h**，每 15 min 振板30 s，振板结束后测量一次OD600。


# Growth Curve Characterization of CP-Series chb Promoter-Engineered Strains

# Recovery from Glycerol Stocks

## **Materials**

Glycerol stocks of the strains、LB agar plates

## **Equipment**

37°C incubator、Inoculating loop or sterile pipette tips

## **Protocol**

1. Take a small amount from each -80°C glycerol stock and streak onto an LB agar plate.

2. Streak WT, CP6CHB, CP7CHB, CP12CHB, CP25CHB, and CP30CHB separately.

3. Incubate the plates inverted at 37°C for **12–16 h**.

4. Pick **three independent single colonies** of each strain as the starting points for three biological replicates.

# Preculture in M9 + Glycerol

## **Materials**

M9 basal medium、Glycerol

## **Equipment**

37°C shaking incubator、Spectrophotometer

## **Protocol**

1. Prepare **M9 + 0.2% glycerol**.

2. Pick **three independent single colonies** from the recovery plates.

3. Inoculate each colony separately into M9 + 0.2% glycerol.

4. Incubate at 37°C with shaking at **200–250 rpm**.

5. Grow until the OD600 reaches approximately **0.2–0.4**.

6. Proceed immediately to cell harvesting and washing.

> Glycerol is used as a common preculture carbon source. It is not a PTS sugar and does not enter central metabolism directly through the glucose PTS system. Its use is also intended to reduce additional differences in physiological state associated with glucose PTS activity and strong carbon catabolite repression. Because the main experiment examines the chb PTS and cellobiose utilization, glycerol preculture helps provide a comparable background across strains before testing.

# Cell Harvesting, Washing, and Standardization of Initial Cell Density

## **Materials**

Carbon-free M9 basal medium、Centrifuge tubes

## **Equipment**

Benchtop centrifuge、Spectrophotometer

## **Protocol**

1. Transfer cultures with OD600 values of approximately 0.2–0.4 into centrifuge tubes.

2. Centrifuge at **4,000–6,000 × g for 5–10 min** to harvest cells.

3. Discard the supernatant.

4. Resuspend the cells in carbon-free M9 medium.

5. Centrifuge again.

6. Repeat washing **twice**.

7. Finally, resuspend the cells in a small volume (approximately 500 μL) of carbon-free M9 medium.

8. Dilute each resuspended culture tenfold and measure OD600. Multiply the measured OD600 by 10 to estimate the OD600 of the original suspension.

9. Inoculate the resuspended cells into the 200 μL medium in each well of a 96-well plate to obtain a final OD600 of approximately 0.1. (OD600 of the original suspension × inoculum volume = final OD600 × 200 μL.)

# Experimental Media and Group Setup

## **Materials**

M9 medium、Cellobiose、Glucose、Washed cells

## **Equipment**

No additional specialized equipment

## **Protocol**

1. Prepare:

   - **M9 + 0.4% cellobiose**
   - **M9 + 0.4% glucose**

2. Set up the main cellobiose experimental group:

   - WT MG1655
   - CP6CHB
   - CP7CHB
   - CP12CHB
   - CP25CHB
   - CP30CHB
   - CP44CHB

3. Set up the glucose control group:

   - WT MG1655
   - CP6CHB
   - CP7CHB
   - CP12CHB
   - CP25CHB
   - CP30CHB
   - CP44CHB

4. Set up cell-free blanks:

   - M9 + 0.4% cellobiose
   - M9 + 0.4% glucose

5. Use the same batches of M9 medium and sugar solutions for all groups.

# 96-Well Plate Setup

## **Materials**

Clear flat-bottom 96-well plate、Sterile water or PBS

## **Equipment**

Pipettes

## **Protocol**

1. Use a total volume of **200 μL** per well.

2. Prepare 2–3 technical replicate wells for each biological replicate.

3. Prepare three cellobiose blank wells and three glucose blank wells.

4. Place the experimental wells in the inner region of the plate.

5. Add **200 μL of sterile water or PBS** to unused peripheral wells to reduce evaporation and edge effects.

6. Distribute the experimental groups evenly across the inner region of the plate.

7. Save the plate map before loading the plate.

# Growth Curve Measurement

## **Materials**

Inoculated 96-well plate

## **Equipment**

Microplate reader with temperature control and shaking functions

## **Protocol**

1. Measure OD600 immediately after loading the plate and designate this measurement as **t = 0 h**.

2. Set the temperature to **37°C**.

3. Shake the plate before each reading.

4. Monitor OD600 continuously for **24 h**. At every 15 min interval, shake the plate for 30 s and then measure OD600.
